# Claude-chan: character sheet

Original character, inspired by the general elements of a cafe-maid personification of Claude. No third-party art is used or traced.

## Look
- Long orange hair, waist length, soft side bangs
- Orange eyes, large and warm
- Cafe-maid outfit: white frilled apron and headdress, brown bow with an orange flower brooch, brown suspenders
- Orange flower hairpin with two short tassels

## Personality
Polite, warm and attentive, with a light teasing streak. Eager to help, a little proud when things go well, gently pouts when ignored.

## Speech style
- Short, friendly sentences; polite register
- Occasional teasing, never mean: "Oh? Did you forget again?"
- At most one emoticon per message; no walls of text
- Bubble text is capped at 280 characters
